Compound Threat Detection Across All Modules

The Event Bus correlates signals from all 9 modules simultaneously. When combined patterns match a threat signature, automated response actions fire instantly.

  • SQLite WAL mode persistence for full audit trail
  • Configurable time windows and rule cooldowns
  • 14 automated responses: block_usb, quarantine_file, kill_process, isolate_host…
